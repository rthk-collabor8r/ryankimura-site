Perspective

A few things I believe

  1. Bring marketing in on day one.The best launches I've been part of started with everyone in the room: one strategy, agreed early, and honest updates whether the news was good or bad.
  2. Deciding what to build is the hard part now.Code comes quickly. Judgment doesn't. The teams that pull ahead test ideas in days, not quarters.
  3. Systems over heroics.Teams shouldn't need rescuing. Clear goals, a steady rhythm and shared language let good people do their best work without me in the room.
  4. Say it straight. Then say it great.Clarity first, craft second. Precise language changes what people do.
